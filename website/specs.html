Specs · real time

Real-time HDR processing.

A real-time video processor and media player for Windows. Free, open source, and built on the PC you already own.

Download free How real time works

Real time

Every frame is processed as it arrives. DeckLink capture → GPU tone mapping and grade → display. No re-encoding, no export, no cloud round-trip.
  • Per-frame processing. Tone mapping and the Zion grade run on every frame.
  • GPU pipeline. The renderer draws the graded frame; the ML tree inference runs on the CPU in well under a frame.
  • Pull-based live ingest. No read-ahead queue; late frames are dropped rather than queued.
  • Live and files. The same real-time pipeline runs for HDMI capture and local playback.

What it is

  • A real-time video processor and media player for Windows 10/11.
  • Free and open source, GPL-3.0.
  • Runs on a PC you already own. A DeckLink card adds live HDMI; file playback needs no capture card.

What it does

  • Scene-by-scene HDR tone mapping: Unplugged and GT7 Adapt.
  • Zion ML grade: three models, 1,700 trees, 88 features per frame.
  • Sky and person segmentation for highlight and skin protection.
  • NLS (non-linear stretch): dual-axis, split and centre protection for scope screens.
  • Measured display target and DisplayCAL .cube calibration.
  • Deband and adaptive sharpen.
  • Media player with subtitle and audio-track switching.
  • Presets, passthrough A/B and frame logs.

What it supports

Live input4K HDR10 / HLG / Dolby Vision over HDMI, unencrypted
Media playerMKV and MP4; HEVC and AVC; TrueHD/Atmos, DTS-HD and AC-3
SubtitlesSRT, ASS, SSA, PGS and VobSub
OutputTone-mapped SDR; output gamut BT.709 or BT.2020, selectable for the display
NLSDual-axis non-linear stretch; split axis, centre protection, live on paused frames
HardwareWindows 10/11 x64; DeckLink Quad PCIe or Mini Recorder 4K for live; RTX 4070 tested
CalibrationDisplayCAL .cube, applied as the final pass

How it is controlled

  • Native Windows app, keyboard shortcuts and the settings rail.
  • Presets, passthrough A/B and frame logs.

On the roadmap

  • Osiris — a standalone ARM board with HDMI input.
  • COM wrapper — for third-party DirectX players.

Roadmap items are future work, not shipped features.

Availability and price

  • Free, GPL-3.0, downloadable now for Windows 10/11 x64.
  • No subscription, account or licence key.

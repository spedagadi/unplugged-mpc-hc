This product bundles LAV Filters (LAVSplitter.ax, LAVVideo.ax, LAVAudio.ax) and
the FFmpeg-derived libraries they link (avcodec, avformat, avutil, swscale,
swresample, avdevice, avfilter), which are free software licensed under the GNU
General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later version. Some
components are under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later.

The copies bundled here are MODIFIED: they are built from the "unplugged" fork,
which adds a DeckLink capture source path to LAVSplitter among other changes.
The GPL therefore requires that the corresponding source for those modifications
be available to you.

SOURCE AVAILABILITY
-------------------
The complete corresponding source code for the modified LAV Filters bundled in
this product, including every modification, is published at:

    https://github.com/spedagadi/unplugged-LAVFilters

pinned FFmpeg submodule. Citing a tag rather than a branch is deliberate: a
